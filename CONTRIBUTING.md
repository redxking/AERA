# Contribution status

Formal public review is not open, and this repository is not accepting issues, pull requests, review comments, implementation evidence, or other contributions.

Contribution, attribution, intellectual-property, patent, privacy, moderation, and disposition terms have not yet been adopted. Do not submit material for incorporation into AERA until a dated public-review notice and contribution process are published here.

Unsolicited submissions may be closed without technical review and do not establish contributor, reviewer, editor, authorship, attribution, licensing, or standards-participation status.

Do not place vulnerabilities, credentials, personal information, controlled information, proprietary material, export-controlled information, classified information, or CUI in an issue or pull request. Security-sensitive matters must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
