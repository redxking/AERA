# Governance status

AERA 1.2 WD is independently authored and currently maintained by Angelis Pseftis as editor and author.

No AERA working group, membership body, certification authority, conformity-assessment program, or multistakeholder consensus process is presently established. Publication in this repository does not confer contributor, reviewer, assessor, implementer, member, or standards-participant status.

## Decision authority

Until a formal governance model is adopted, the author retains editorial and publication authority for AERA. Proposed requirements remain developmental and do not become consensus requirements through repository activity.

## Change control

- The authoritative human-readable representation is identified in the release manifest.
- Human-readable and machine-readable derivatives must remain semantically consistent.
- Every controlled artifact is bound to an edition and build identifier.
- Published build artifacts are not silently replaced. A byte-changing replacement requires updated integrity records; a normative change requires a new specification version.
- Assessment independence, review participation, dissent, conflicts of interest, pilot evidence, and comment dispositions must be disclosed before related claims are made.

## External-status boundary

AERA is not a publication, standard, profile, certification, or endorsement of NIST, ISO, IEC, IEEE, CISA, DoD, DOE, another government body, or another standards organization. External citations and mappings are informative and do not establish reciprocal compliance, adoption, correctness, or support.
