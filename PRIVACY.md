# Privacy notice

Formal public review and contribution intake are not open. Do not submit personal data or sensitive information through repository issues, pull requests, discussions, or files.

Interactions with this public repository may expose a GitHub account name, profile, timestamp, content, and activity history under GitHub's platform terms and privacy practices. AERA does not currently operate a separate review database or authorized intake channel.

Do not submit credentials, private contact details, health or employment information, proprietary data, export-controlled information, classified information, CUI, or information about another person without authority to disclose it.

Identity-publication choices, retention, correction, withdrawal, attribution, and deletion procedures will be defined before a formal review period opens.
